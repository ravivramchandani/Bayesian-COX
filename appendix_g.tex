\chapter{GBSG2 Horseshoe Diagnostics and Sampler Sensitivity}\label{appendix-g-gbsg2-horseshoe-failure-and-recovery}\label{app:horseshoe}

The original Horseshoe model met the diagnostic criteria in seven of 25 GBSG2 folds, 23 of 25 METABRIC folds and all 25 WHAS500 folds. A separate post-hoc sensitivity changed target acceptance to 0.999 across all 75 Horseshoe folds, retaining the original data partitions, seeds, refit rules and diagnostic criteria. It accepted all 75 fits. Table~\ref{tab:gen-sampler-sensitivity} reports its nine paired prior contrasts against the unchanged comparators.
