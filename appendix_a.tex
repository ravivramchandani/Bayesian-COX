\section{Analysis Populations and Source Predictors}
The analysis includes 686 GBSG2 participants, 500 WHAS500 participants and 1,980 METABRIC participants. GBSG2 records recurrence-free survival in days; WHAS500 records all-cause mortality in days; METABRIC records overall survival in months. Each cohort is analysed on its own endpoint and time scale.

Table~\ref{tab:gen-predictor-missingness} lists the clinical source predictors before categorical expansion and training-fold preprocessing. Numeric medians, scaling factors, category levels and reference categories are estimated within each training partition. METABRIC stage is included in the stage and multiple-imputation sensitivity specifications; its 514 missing values are handled within those analyses.
