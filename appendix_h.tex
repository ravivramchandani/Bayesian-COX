\section{Paired Bootstrap Sensitivity of IBS Contrasts}

Table~\ref{tab:ibs-bootstrap-sensitivity} reports the saved sensitivity estimates for the five complete original prior contrasts. The bootstrap resampled validation patients within paired folds, holding the fitted predictions fixed. It therefore describes a different source of variation from the corrected repeated-validation interval. The original primary inference retains the corrected interval and its complete-fold and multiplicity rules.
