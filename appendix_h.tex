Calibration summaries are reported at the 25th, 50th and 75th percentiles of outer-training event times. Calibration-in-the-large is the complementary-log--log intercept fitted with the prediction term as an offset. The joint calibration model estimates both intercept and slope, whose ideal values are zero and one. Tables~\ref{tab:calibration-all-gbsg2}--\ref{tab:calibration-all-metabric} report fold means and standard deviations, with the number of accepted folds for each model.
