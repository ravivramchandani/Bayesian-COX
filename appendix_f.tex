\chapter{Detailed Robustness Estimates}
\label{app:sensitivity}

The robustness analyses vary training information, administrative censoring, baseline interval count, prior scale and predictor specification. Table~\ref{tab:gen-robustness-effects} contains all 129 within-condition contrasts and 45 difference-in-differences contrasts. Each row records its paired-fold coverage and bootstrap interval.

A direct effect is IBS($A$) minus IBS($B$) in the stated condition. A difference-in-differences subtracts the corresponding prior contrast under full training and observed follow-up. Thus a positive Normal-minus-horseshoe difference-in-differences indicates a larger relative horseshoe benefit under the specified information change. Intervals use 2,000 paired three-stage bootstrap replications over repeats, folds and validation subjects.
