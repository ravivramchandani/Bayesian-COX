\section{Training-Data Predictive Checks}

Table~\ref{tab:sup-predictive-checks} summarises the saved checks from the first accepted attempt in each Bayesian outer fit. Total and interval-specific event counts were compared with Poisson replications at the observed exposures. Separately, training Kaplan--Meier estimates were compared with posterior intervals for mean fitted survival at three training-event quartiles. These quantities describe count replication and fitted-survival agreement, respectively; they were reported separately from the MCMC acceptance criteria.
