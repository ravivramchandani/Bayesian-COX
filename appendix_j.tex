\chapter{Prior Sensitivity and Computational Reliability}\label{appendix-j-prior-sensitivity-and-final-computational-integrity}\label{app:robustness}

The robustness analysis varied training information, follow-up truncation, baseline intervals, prior scales, predictors and preprocessing. It comprised 825 condition-specific outcomes, of which 770 met the diagnostic criteria. Training-size and censoring comparisons use ten outer pairs when complete; design and feature comparisons use five. Intervals use 2,000 paired hierarchical bootstrap replicates.
