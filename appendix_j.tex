\chapter{Prior Sensitivity and Computational Reliability}\label{appendix-j-prior-sensitivity-and-final-computational-integrity}\label{app:robustness}

The robustness analysis varied training information, follow-up truncation, baseline intervals, prior scales, predictors and preprocessing. It comprised 825 model--condition--fold outcomes, of which 770 were accepted. These outcomes involved 885 underlying Bayesian fits. A multiple-imputation outcome required all five imputation-specific fits to pass the diagnostic criteria; their subject-level survival predictions were then averaged. Training-size and censoring comparisons use ten outer pairs when complete; design and feature comparisons use five. Intervals use 2,000 paired hierarchical bootstrap replicates.
