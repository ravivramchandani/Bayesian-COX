# Independent literature consistency check

Date: 17 November 2026. Read-only review of the completed `literature_review.tex`, with focused source checks against the primary PDF/XML caches, `reference_recovery_audit.json` and the implemented methodology. No thesis source was edited during this review.

## Two concrete corrections identified and resolved

The literature agent applied both corrections below during the independent check. Their corrected wording was re-read in the current chapter and verified. No further substantive correction is outstanding from this review.

1. **Line 97, BHCox baseline versus heredity.** The sentence “Its M-spline baseline and heredity structure link interaction effects to their main effects” gives the baseline and heredity structure the same role. The baseline represents the underlying hazard; the heredity constraints connect interaction effects with main effects. Suggested replacement: “Its baseline hazard uses M-splines, while heredity constraints link interaction effects to their main effects.” The surrounding distinction between BHCox, BHAFT and the present main-effects comparison is accurate. Source: `lit_pdf_text/Sun_2025_BHCox-Bayesian-heredity-constrained-Cox-proportional-hazards-models-for-detecting-gene-environment.txt:160–218`, and the corresponding `paper_text/ref52_PMC11834309.xml` Methods. The BHAFT AFT distinction is supported by the publisher/author abstract recorded for `ref68` in the source audit.

2. **Line 149, Objective 4, diagnostic ordering.** “Applying the predefined diagnostic criteria before posterior inference and predictive comparison” is temporally inaccurate: posterior sampling/inference produces the draws that are diagnosed. The gate precedes posterior interpretation and predictive comparison. Replace “before posterior inference” with “before posterior interpretation”. This matches `project/src/models/bayesian/model.py:231–249`, `project/src/pipelines/confirmatory/fitting.py:259–318` and the corrected methods/figure caption.

## Focused checks that require no correction

- **Lines 71–75, prediction targets:** Nunez uses binary survival labels at 6/36/60 months (`paper_text/ref11_PMC9972192.xml`, Methods); Guo classifies survival beyond five years using gene-expression, copy-number and clinical inputs (`paper_text/ref48_PMC8417828.xml`, Materials). Their treatment as horizon classification rather than direct survival-curve benchmarks is accurate. The discrete-time versus piecewise-exponential distinction is consistent with Suresh's full-text treatment of person-period models and the actual likelihood.
- **Lines 97–103, prior comparison:** BHCox includes heredity-constrained spike-and-slab/regularised-horseshoe PH models with an M-spline baseline; BHAFT is AFT. The present study is appropriately described as a clinical main-effects comparison with a common piecewise-exponential baseline and Normal reference. The full-prior-configuration and continuous Beta variance-weight distinctions agree with the model code. No claim of first comparing the prior families remains.
- **Line 99, spatial/rank-based Bayesian methods:** Song's partial-likelihood description agrees with the full supplied article text in `paper_text/ref94_Consensus_fulltext.txt`; Mu's two-stage site-specific partial-likelihood then MCMC description agrees with its primary author manuscript (`lit_pdf_text/Mu_2021_Bayesian-variable-selection-for-Cox-regression-model-with-spatially-varying-coefficients-with.txt:18–25,83–87`).
- **Lines 119–127, explanation targets:** The current text distinguishes survival probabilities/curves, relative mortality risk and predicted event times. Intrinsic posterior contribution importance and centred SHAP of the posterior-mean log-risk predictor are now described separately. In the current line 127, “for the posterior-mean log-risk predictor from the same Bayesian model” is consistent with the methodology; the earlier “same posterior-mean target” wording is no longer present.
- **Lines 131–171, synthesis/objectives/RQs:** The contribution is framed as an integrated empirical comparison rather than a new survival model or new prior. RQ4 separates predictive perturbation sensitivity from attribution sampling stability. This is consistent with the 825-condition predictive/diagnostic robustness programme and the separately implemented explanation-budget/seed analysis.

Only the two corrections above are recommended; this check does not justify another structural rewrite.
