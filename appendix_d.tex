\chapter{Bayesian Model Specification and Prior Structures}\label{appendix-d-bayesian-model-specification-and-prior-structures}\label{app:model-specification}

The three Bayesian arms share the piecewise-exponential likelihood and the independent baseline log-hazard prior $\log\lambda_j\sim\mathcal{N}(-2,2^2)$. Their coefficient priors are specified in Section~\ref{bayesian-prior-specification}, including the scale interpretation of the continuous variance mixture. Table~\ref{tab:prior-configuration} collects their hyperparameters; $j$ indexes baseline intervals and $k$ indexes coefficients.
